Before creating a canvas app is
important to consider if a model driven
app is better suited. Here are some
factors to consider. Customization
versus standardization. If you need a
highly customized user interface, canvas
apps are the way to go. For standardized
datadriven applications, model driven
apps are better suited. Complexity of
business processes. Model driven apps
excel in handling complex business
processes and workflows. While canvas
apps offer more flexibility for simple
usercentric applications.
In conclusion, the battle of canvas apps
versus model driven apps. There is no
oneizefits-all answer. Each approach has
its own strengths and is better suited
for different scenarios.

the capabilities of each app type, you
